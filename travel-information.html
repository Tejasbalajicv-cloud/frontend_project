Travel Information

How to reach, when to go, what to pack and a five-day plan.

How to reach

By air

  • Kempegowda International Airport (BLR), Bengaluru - the main gateway, connected to Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata and international cities.
  • Mysore (MYQ), Mangalore (IXE) and Hubballi (HBX) airports run a few domestic flights.
  • From Bengaluru airport to the city it takes about an hour by road.

By train

  • KSR Bengaluru (SBC), Yesvantpur (YPR), Mysuru Junction (MYS), Hubballi (UBI) and Mangaluru Junction (MAQ) are the big stations.
  • Vande Bharat and Shatabdi connect Bengaluru with Mysuru, Hubballi and Chennai.
  • Book on IRCTC; overnight trains to Hampi (Hospet station, HPT) fill up fast in season.

By road

  • State-run KSRTC buses (including Airavata and Grande Volvo) run everywhere and are the cheapest option.
  • National highways NH-48, NH-66 and NH-75 link the main towns.
  • Self-drive is easy on the coast and in the Ghats; carry a paper map as signal drops in the forests.

Getting around

  • KSRTC and private Volvo buses are the backbone - book a night bus Bengaluru to Coorg or Gokarna and save a hotel.
  • Trains are cheap and scenic on the Mysuru-Bengaluru and Hubballi-Gadag lines; the Ghats section has tunnels and bridges.
  • Autos and app cabs work in Bengaluru, Mysuru and Mangalore; in hill towns pre-paid taxi stands or a rented scooter is simpler.
  • In Hampi, auto-rickshaws and cycles are the only way around; cars cannot enter the core ruins.
  • Forest areas such as Bandipur close at night, so plan safaris early morning or late evening.

What to pack

  • Cotton clothes and a light jacket - hill mornings can drop to 10 degrees even in December.
  • A good pair of shoes with grip for rocks, temple steps and monsoon trails.
  • Sunscreen, a cap and a reusable bottle; the plateau sun is strong year-round.
  • Modest clothing for temples - shoulders and knees covered; some temples ask men to remove shirts.
  • Power bank and cash; small towns and forest check-posts still prefer rupee notes.

Travel tips

  • Carry a photo ID - it is checked at forest entry and for some permits.
  • Photography is banned inside most sanctuaries and inside the Mysore Palace halls.
  • Ask before photographing people, and never step inside a roped-off monument area.
  • Learn three Kannada words and people warm up instantly: Namaskaara (hello), Dhanyavaadagalu (thank you), Ekku thriska (how much).
  • Tap water in small towns - stick to bottled or filtered water and skip ice on the coast in peak summer.

Five-day plan

Day 1Arrive Bengaluru. Lalbagh botanical garden, Cubbon Park and the State Museum, then a Mysore masala dosa dinner.
Day 2Drive to Mysuru (3 hours). Mysore Palace, Chamundi Hill, Devaraja Market and St. Philomena's Church. Night in Mysuru.
Day 3Mysuru to Coorg through Bandipur (4 hours). Elephant or tiger safari in the morning, coffee estate walk and Raja's Seat sunset.
Day 4Coorg to Chikmagalur. Mullayanagiri viewpoint, Hebbe Falls trek and an evening in a coffee-estate homestay.
Day 5Chikmagalur to Jog Falls (3 hours), then onward to Gokarna for the beach and a simple fish-thali dinner.

Common questions

How many days are enough for Karnataka?

Five days covers Bengaluru, Mysuru, Coorg and one waterfall. Ten days lets you add Hampi, Bijapur and the beaches.

Is Karnataka safe for solo and family travellers?

Yes. Cities and tourist towns are well policed, KSRTC buses are reliable and homestays are used to solo travellers. Use normal city sense at night.

Do I need a permit for the hills or forests?

Bandipur and Nagarhole safaris need a ticket bought at the gate or online. Kudremukh and some Anamalai-trek routes need a forest permit in advance.

What is the cost of a week-long trip?

A comfortable budget traveller spends about INR 2,500 to 4,000 a day including a homestay, meals, local transport and one safari.

Which is the best month for Jog Falls?

July to December, when the Sharavathi is in full flood. In summer the four streams reduce to thin threads.
