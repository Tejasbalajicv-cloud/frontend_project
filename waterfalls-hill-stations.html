Waterfalls & Hill Stations

Falls and cool hills of the Western Ghats.

Waterfalls

Jog Falls

Shivamogga district 253 m Best: Jun to Dec

The Sharavathi River drops in four named streams - Raja, Rani, Rocket and Rookie - into a green gorge.

H

Hogenakkal Falls

Dharmapuri / border of Karnataka 20 m Best: Jul to Jan

Carbonatite rock cliffs on the Kaveri, famous for basket rides and coral sand.

S

Shivanasamudra Falls

Mandya district 98 m Best: Aug to Dec

The Kaveri splits into two, Gaganachukki and Bharachukki, beside Asia's first hydro power station.

M

Magod Falls

Uttara Kannada 200 m Best: Jul to Dec

A twin-horsehoe drop in the Bedti river, far from crowds and easy to reach by road.

H

Hebbe Falls

Chikkamagaluru district 168 m Best: Jun to Jan

A trek through coffee estate and forest above the Kemmanagundi range.

Hill stations

Coorg (Kodagu)

Kodagu About 1,525 m Best: Sep to Mar

Coffee estates, Abbey and Iruppu falls, Raja's Seat sunset and the homestay capital of the south.

C

Chikmagalur

Chikkamagaluru About 1,090 m Best: Oct to Mar

Where coffee was first planted in India, with Mullayanagiri as the highest peak in the state.

K

Kudremukh

Chikkamagaluru About 1,894 m Best: Oct to May

Shola grasslands and lion-tailed macaques; the famous trek needs a forest permit.

A

Agumbe

Shivamogga district About 850 m Best: Sep to Feb

The 'Cherrapunji of the south' - king cobras, clouds and one of the Western Ghats' richest forests.

N

Nandi Hills

Chikkaballapur About 1,478 m Best: Nov to Feb

The easiest weekend climb, best for a sunrise above the clouds and a bowl of bajji on the top.

B

BR Hills & Sangameshwara

Chamarajanagar About 1,226 m Best: Oct to Mar

A quiet tiger reserve hill with the Biligiriranga temple and the river confluence at the foot.
