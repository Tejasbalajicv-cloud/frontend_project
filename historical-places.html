Historical Places

Temples, forts and palaces built by the great rulers of Karnataka.

Badami Cave Temples

6th century CE Badami Chalukyas Badami, Vijayapura district

Four temples cut straight into red sandstone cliffs, with carvings of Shiva, Vishnu and Jain Tirthankaras.

Aihole & Pattadakal

7th - 8th century CE Early & Later Chalukyas Bagalkot district

Pattadakal is a UNESCO site: temples that mix north and south Indian styles in one courtyard.

Chennakeshava Temple

12th century CE Hoysala Empire Belur, Hassan district

Soapstone walls covered with dancers, elephants and epics, carved so finely they look like wood.

Hampi Group of Monuments

14th - 16th century CE Vijayanagara Empire Hospet, Ballari district

The capital city of Vijayanagara: Virupaksha Temple, the stone chariot, Hazara Rama and a mile of stone bazaars.

Gol Gumbaz

1656 CE Adil Shahi dynasty Vijayapura (Bijapur)

One of the largest domes in the world, famous for a seven-fold echo in the gallery above the tomb.

M

Mysore Palace

1912 CE Wodeyar dynasty Mysuru

Built after the old wooden palace burned; the Durbar Hall and the golden howdah are the highlights.

D

Daulat Bagh & Summer Palace

18th century CE Hyder Ali & Tipu Sultan Srirangapatna, Mandya district

Tipu Sultan's wooden summer palace and the battle site where the Fourth Anglo-Mysore War ended in 1799.
